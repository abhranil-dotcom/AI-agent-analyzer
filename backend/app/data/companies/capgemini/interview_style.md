## Interview Style Summary

Capgemini interviews weight **communication/client-facing polish alongside technical
fundamentals** more than most IT-services peers, reflecting its international client base
(France, Germany, UK, US). Expect standard HR + technical-fundamentals + project-discussion
rounds, with technical bar moderate for freshers but scaling up meaningfully for lateral SAP,
cloud, or architecture roles. Coding bar is Easy-to-Medium at the fresher level. No system-design
or named-leadership-principle round at this level.
