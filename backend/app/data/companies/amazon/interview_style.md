## Interview Style Summary

Amazon interviews are weighted toward **Leadership Principles-mapped behavioral questions +
deep technical/coding**. Every round, including technical ones, includes behavioral questions
explicitly mapped to Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles (see the `leadership_principles` knowledge
category), and strong answers use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) with a real,
specific example — never a hypothetical. Technical bar is high: strong DSA fundamentals plus
system-design depth for SDE II and above. A Bar Raiser interviewer protects this bar across every
loop, so hr_questions here should lean explicitly into named-principle framing rather than generic
behavioral phrasing.
