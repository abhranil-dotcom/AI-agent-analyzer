## Interview Style Summary

TCS interviews are weighted toward **HR + Technical fundamentals + project discussion**, with a
mass-scale online assessment (NQT) as the biggest filter before any human interview happens.
Coding bar is Easy-to-Medium — clarity and correctness matter far more than micro-optimized
solutions. Expect roughly equal time on core CS fundamentals, the primary language/project on the
resume, and HR/culture-fit topics (relocation, shift flexibility, communication). System design and
leadership-principle-style behavioral mapping are not part of TCS's bar at the entry level this kit
targets.
