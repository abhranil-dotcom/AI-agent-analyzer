## TCS NQT (Online Assessment) Pattern

The TCS National Qualifier Test (NQT) is a proctored online assessment and the single biggest
filter in TCS's fresher hiring funnel — most candidates who don't clear an interview round were
actually eliminated here, not in the interview itself.

### Sections and structure
- **Numerical Ability**: percentages, ratios, profit/loss, time-speed-distance, and data
  interpretation from tables/graphs. Moderate difficulty, time pressure is the main challenge.
- **Verbal Ability**: reading comprehension, sentence correction/completion, and synonyms/antonyms.
- **Reasoning Ability**: logical puzzles, series completion, blood relations, coding-decoding, and
  syllogisms.
- **Programming Logic / MCQs**: fundamentals of OOP, data structures, and output-prediction
  questions in C/C++/Java/Python.
- **Coding section**: typically 1-2 coding problems (Easy-to-Medium, e.g. array/string
  manipulation, basic recursion) to be solved in a language of the candidate's choice, auto-graded
  against test cases.

### What it filters for
Aptitude and basic coding ability rather than deep CS knowledge — the bar is calibrated for a very
large, diverse campus applicant pool, so consistent accuracy under time pressure matters more than
tackling the hardest possible problem. Candidates who clear NQT are assumed trainable and move to
the technical + HR interview rounds described in `interview_process`.

### Preparation approach
Timed mock tests for numerical/verbal/reasoning sections, plus basic-to-medium coding practice
(arrays, strings, recursion, simple DP) are the highest-leverage prep for this stage — depth on any
single topic matters less than broad, fast, accurate coverage.
