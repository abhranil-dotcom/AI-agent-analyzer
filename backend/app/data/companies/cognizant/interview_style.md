## Interview Style Summary

Cognizant interviews weight **domain adaptability (healthcare/BFSI) alongside core technical
fundamentals** — most freshers enter through the GenC program, with GenC Pro/Elevate and lateral
tracks carrying a noticeably higher technical bar. Standard shape: HR + technical fundamentals +
project discussion, Easy-to-Medium coding bar for GenC, higher for GenC Pro/Elevate and lateral
roles. Comfort with regulated, process-heavy environments (healthcare compliance, BFSI risk/fraud
systems) is a recurring HR-round theme. No system-design or named-leadership-principle round at
this level.
