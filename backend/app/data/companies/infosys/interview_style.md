## Interview Style Summary

Infosys interviews are weighted toward **HR + Technical fundamentals + project/training-pipeline
fit**, similar in shape to TCS: a proctored online assessment (aptitude, verbal, logical reasoning,
and a coding section) filters the bulk of campus candidates before a technical and HR round.
Coding bar is Easy-to-Medium, with extra credit for candidates who've done InfyTQ certifications
or side projects showing initiative. HR rounds probe adaptability to Infosys's long structured
onboarding (Mysore training campus) and willingness to work across its broad service-line
portfolio (Java/.NET/SAP/cloud). No system-design or named-leadership-principle round at this
level.
