## Interview Style Summary

Google interviews are weighted toward **DSA + problem-solving**, deliberately fundamentals-heavy
and framework-agnostic — coding rounds focus on algorithms, data structures, and rigorous
complexity analysis rather than any specific language or stack, on the theory that strong
fundamentals transfer across any tech Google uses internally. Coding bar is Medium-to-Hard with
unusually strong emphasis on clean code and clear verbal reasoning while solving, not just reaching
the right answer. A distinctive "Googleyness and Leadership" round assesses culture fit and comfort
with ambiguity — hr_questions should reflect that framing rather than a generic HR-round tone.
