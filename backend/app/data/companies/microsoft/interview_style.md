## Interview Style Summary

Microsoft interviews are weighted toward **Coding + System Design + Behavioural ("growth
mindset")**. Coding rounds cover core CS fundamentals and role-relevant DSA at a Medium bar.
System design is weighted heavily for SDE II and above, less so for new grads — when generating
technical questions for a mid/senior-leaning role, fold in system-design questions (see the
`system_design` knowledge category); for a clearly entry-level role, keep system design light or
omit it. Behavioral questions should explicitly probe growth-mindset traits: genuine curiosity,
comfort with being wrong, and collaborative (not competitive) framing of past experiences — this is
Microsoft's named cultural pillar and shows up directly in HR-style questions.
