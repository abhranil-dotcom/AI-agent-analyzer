## Interview Style Summary

Accenture interviews weight **technology-business versatility and communication** more heavily
than pure delivery-shop peers, since much of its work is consulting-adjacent — candidates are
often expected to reason about a technology choice's business impact, not just its technical
correctness. Otherwise the shape is standard: HR + technical fundamentals + project discussion,
Easy-to-Medium coding bar for freshers, scaling to moderate-to-high for lateral cloud/data/AI and
architecture roles. No system-design or named-leadership-principle round at this level.
